Flux Hourglass · com.flux.hourglass

Privacy Policy

Flux Hourglass collects nothing, transmits nothing, and shares nothing. This page documents that in plain language, in English and Korean.

English

1. Summary

Flux Hourglass (the “App”, package com.flux.hourglass) is an offline Android timer. It does not collect, store off-device, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data of any kind.

2. Data we collect

None. Specifically:

  • No account, sign-up, or login.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage metrics.
  • No advertising SDKs, no ad identifiers, no tracking pixels.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, calendar, files, or device identifiers are read.
  • No third-party SDK that performs data collection is bundled in the app.

3. Data we store on your device

The App writes a single small preference file on your device that remembers the last hours / minutes / seconds you started a timer with, so the pickers are pre-seeded on the next launch. This data:

  • Stays on your device in the app’s private storage.
  • Is never transmitted off the device.
  • Is removed automatically when you uninstall the App or clear its storage from Android Settings.

4. Network access

The App does not request the INTERNET permission and makes no network requests of any kind. It cannot contact a server even if it wanted to.

5. Permissions

The App requests exactly one Android permission: android.permission.VIBRATE, used to vibrate the device when the timer finishes. No other permissions are declared or requested.

6. Children’s privacy

Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children. It is suitable for users of all ages.

7. Third parties

The App contains no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics, and no third-party social SDKs. The only third party involved in distribution is Google Play itself, which is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new “Last updated” date below. Material changes will be reflected in the App’s release notes on Google Play.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to okpos.cs1.2@gmail.com.
