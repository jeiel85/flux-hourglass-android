# Flux Hourglass v1.10.0 Release Notes

**Flux Hourglass v1.10.0** refocuses the app on its hourglass identity by cutting 11 half-baked visualizer modes and raising the quality of the four that remain: **Sand, Water, Fire, LED**.

## Why

The app started as a single particle-physics hourglass, then accumulated 14 more "visualizer" modes over six releases. Several were thin demos — static scenes redrawn at 60 fps, modes that registered the accelerometer but never used it, a "Flip" mode that didn't flip, an "Ink" mode whose dark-blue dots were nearly invisible on the black background. The cross-cutting features (soundscapes, sandbox settings, obstacle drawing) only worked in 2–3 of the 15 modes, so most of the selector felt broken.

This release trims the lineup to the four modes that are genuinely finished and makes the shared features coherent across them.

## Removed — 11 modes

Nebula, Moss, Ink, Crystal, Wax, Flip, Magnetic, Aurora, Rain, Black Hole, and Electric are gone. `MainActivity.kt` shrank from 4,863 lines to 2,746. Users who had one of these modes persisted as their last selection automatically fall back to **Sand** on next launch via `DisplayMode.parse`.

## Changed

### LED — liquid-level dot matrix

LED previously registered the accelerometer and loaded calibration but never used either — pure dead code. It's now a **liquid-level dot matrix**:

- The 16×32 grid still fills bottom-up by elapsed time, but each column's fill level is offset by the live tilt, so the lit surface **tilts like a real liquid** when you tip the phone.
- Boundary cells (partially filled) carry a **subtle pulse** so the surface feels alive.
- The dead sensor/calibration code is removed; the accelerometer is now actually wired in.
- The sound toggle is hidden in LED since it has no soundscape.

### Sand — falling-grain soundscape

Procedural audio now synthesizes a **soft falling-grain sound** for Sand — low-pass filtered noise at low amplitude with rare faint grain ticks. It joins the existing campfire (Fire) and wave (Water) sounds. The generic hum fallback that played for every mode without a dedicated sound is removed.

### Fire — respects the Sandbox gravity setting

Fire now reads `LocalSandboxSettings` and applies `gravityScale` to flame rise, ember turbulence, and tilt bias. The SETT panel's Gravity Sensitivity slider is now meaningful in Fire (behavior at 1.0× is unchanged). The Sandbox settings previously only affected Sand and Water.

## Migration

- Saved durations and the last-selected mode are preserved. A persisted mode that no longer exists maps to Sand.
- Sandbox settings, calibration, and obstacle drawing are unchanged and still apply to Sand and Water.
- versionCode 11 → 12, versionName 1.9.1 → 1.10.0.
