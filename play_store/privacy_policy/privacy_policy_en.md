# Flux Hourglass Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-27

Flux Hourglass (the "App") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from its users.

## 1. Data we collect

The App collects **no user data** of any kind.

- No personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- No location data
- No access to contacts, photos, or device storage
- No camera or microphone access
- No advertising identifier (AAID), device ID, or install ID collection
- No analytics, no crash reporting
- No payment information (the App is free and contains no in-app purchases)

## 2. Permissions

The only Android permission the App requests is `VIBRATE`, used solely to play a short vibration pulse when an hourglass timer completes. No identifying information is gathered through this permission.

The App does **not** request the `INTERNET` permission and never communicates with any server.

## 3. On-device preferences

For user convenience the App stores **only the last HR / MIN / SEC values you started a timer with** in Android's on-device `Preferences DataStore`. These values are used to pre-fill the picker on next launch and never leave the device. Uninstalling the App removes them.

## 4. Third-party SDKs

The App contains **no** third-party SDKs of any kind, including:

- No advertising SDKs
- No analytics SDKs
- No crash-reporting SDKs
- No push-notification SDKs
- No in-app billing SDKs
- No social login SDKs

## 5. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from any user, including children.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the App's GitHub repository (https://github.com/jeiel85/flux-hourglass-android) and on its landing page (https://jeiel85.github.io/flux-hourglass-android/).
