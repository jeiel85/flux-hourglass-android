One particle-physics hourglass on a pitch-black background. That's it.

No ads. No network. No analytics. The only permission is VIBRATE.

■ Highlights

• Particle-physics hourglass — every grain is an independent particle that falls, collides, and is absorbed into a pile growing toward the bottom. The pile reaches toward half the screen as the timer ends.

• Tilt-aware — the accelerometer feeds a live gravity vector into the simulation. Tip the phone and the pile pours toward the lower edge. Works flat on a desk, in your hand, at any angle.

• Touch to reveal time — leave it alone and you only see sand. Press anywhere on the running screen and exact HH : MM : SS fades in. Release to return to pure sand.

• Quick presets — one tap on 1m / 3m / 5m / 10m / 25m / 1h fills the HR / MIN / SEC pickers. Your usual timer is two taps away.

• Pause / Resume — stop mid-fall and pick up exactly where you left off. The paused screen shows remaining time plus percent left, both in the same minimal type.

• Remembers your last duration — the HR / MIN / SEC you last started with is preloaded on next launch. Re-running the same timer takes a single tap.

• Soft completion chime — a three-note tone rides the notification stream alongside the vibration pulse, so it respects silent and Do Not Disturb.

• Keep-screen-on while running — the screen stays on only during a running timer. The flag clears the instant you pause, finish, or reset.

■ Made for

• Meditation and breathwork — replace the alarm screen with a falling landscape.
• Pomodoro focus blocks — double-tap the 25m chip to start.
• Bedtime 5 minutes, tea steep, workout rest — perceive time visually, without a screen full of widgets.

■ Permissions / Privacy

• No INTERNET permission. The app never contacts any server.
• No ad SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK.
• No location, camera, microphone, or storage requests.
• VIBRATE is the only permission used, and only for a short pulse on completion.

■ Compatibility

• Android 7.0 (API 24) and up.
• Tilt response on any device with an accelerometer (i.e., almost every phone and tablet).
• Dark-mode by design — the background is always pitch black, independent of system dark mode.

This app is a personal project by a single developer and collects no user data.
