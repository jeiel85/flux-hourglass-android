Quick-pick presets, pause/resume, screen stays on while sand falls.

• Quick presets — tap 1m / 3m / 5m / 10m / 25m / 1h above the pickers to set
  a duration instantly.
• Pause and resume — a faint P A U S E control during a run freezes the
  countdown; R E S U M E picks it back up where the sand left off.
• Display stays on while the timer is running, so the hourglass never sleeps.
• Paused view shows exact remaining time and percent left at a glance.
