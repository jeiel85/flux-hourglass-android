v1.0.0 Flux Hourglass — first release

The hourglass timer
• Particle physics — each grain falls and piles up to half the screen as time elapses
• Tilt the phone to make the pile slump under real gravity via the accelerometer
• Press and hold the screen to reveal exact remaining time
• Drag or tap the H R / M I N / S E C pickers to set a duration

Design principles
• Pure black-and-white surface, no ads, no analytics
• Only the VIBRATE permission, no network access
