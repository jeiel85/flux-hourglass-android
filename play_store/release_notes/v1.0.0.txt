Initial release of Flux Hourglass — a minimalist particle-physics hourglass timer.

• Set a duration with the H R / M I N / S E C drag pickers.
• Watch real sand particles fall and pile up as time elapses.
• Tilt the device to slump the pile under physical gravity.
• Touch and hold to reveal the exact remaining time.
• Pure black-and-white design, no network, no analytics, no ads.
