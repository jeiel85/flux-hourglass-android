Gentle completion chime and the timer now remembers your last duration.

• A soft three-note chime plays when the hourglass finishes, in addition to
  the existing vibration pulse. Volume is intentionally subdued so it does
  not intrude during focus work.
• Your last H R / M I N / S E C is remembered between launches, so opening
  the app puts you one tap away from the timer you used last time.
• Quick preset chips remain unchanged for one-tap durations.
