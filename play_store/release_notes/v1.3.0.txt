<en-US>
v1.3.0 Sand fills 100%, gyro response, LED mode

What's new
• LED grid mode — a new timer that fills the screen with a 16×32 LED grid. Switch via the SAND / LED tab at the top of setup; your choice is saved
• Gyro response — tilt the device and falling sand curves with gravity while the piled sand immediately slumps toward the low side

Improved
• Sand fills 100% — when the timer ends the screen is fully covered. Previously the pile capped at two thirds
• Smoother texture — smaller grains and almost twice the columns (now 90) for a much finer surface
• Bolder typography — faint secondary labels gained brightness and weight
</en-US>
